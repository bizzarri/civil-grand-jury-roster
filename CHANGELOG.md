# Changelog

## 1.11

- Added an in-app county test email editor that shows the recipient and starts with the saved template.
- Hands the edited draft to the email app for review and sending.

## 1.10

- Added member contact actions for Android Contacts, the phone dialer, and county test email.
- Added a configurable test email body that persists in encrypted handoffs.

## 1.9 — 2026-11-07

- Added Grand Jury Room key issued to member onboarding, with seven-item progress totals.
- Added README instructions for installing an APK, loading a court spreadsheet or encrypted handoff, updating the app and optional USB installation.

## 1.8

- Added Form 700 filed and Grand Jury Badge obtained to member onboarding.
- Preserved existing marks and updated Work progress totals to use the configured check count.
- Added save/handoff regression coverage for the additional checks.

## 1.7 — 2026-10-30

- Exact term start/end dates in Config > App & Term, with calendar pickers and handoff persistence.
- Older rosters gain July 1–June 30 defaults based on their saved term years.

## 1.6

- Meeting-day roster checkboxes for in-person and online attendance; choosing one clears the other.
- Both modes count as present and persist in meeting history, CSV and encrypted handoffs.
- Existing unspecified Present records remain supported.

## 1.5

- Meetings and attendance under Work, preserving the five existing navigation tabs.
- Configurable weekday/occurrence schedule, preview, individual dates, rescheduling, cancellation and removal.
- Persistent exceptions prevent regeneration from reviving skipped dates.
- Historical participant snapshots, editable attendance, attendance CSV and encrypted handoff support.
- Removed meetings retain attendance history and may be restored.

## 1.4

- Bulk oath/orientation completion with role selection, member preview and per-member history.

## 1.3 — initial public source baseline

- Regular jurors, alternates, departures and vacancies for one term.
- Personal and county email tracking, verification states and suggested county addresses.
- Onboarding, operational notes and distribution-list work.
- XLSX/CSV import review preserving operational updates.
- Configurable display name, term years, title slots and roster filters.
- CSV export and encrypted leadership handoff.

Public packaging excludes operational records, private workbook fixtures, device captures and signing keys. This deposit publishes source; it does not publish a prebuilt APK or change the existing installed app.
